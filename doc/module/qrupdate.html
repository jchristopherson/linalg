Interfaces
DCH1DN DCH1UP DQR1UP ZCH1DN ZCH1UP ZQR1UP

A module providing explicit interfaces for the QRUPDATE library. The routines support rank-one updates and downdates for QR factorizations in both real and complex arithmetic.
